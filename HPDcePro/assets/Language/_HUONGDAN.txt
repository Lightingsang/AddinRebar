HUONG DAN SUA BAN DICH - DCE Excel
=========================================================================

1. CHON NGON NGU
   Tren thanh ribbon DceAddin -> nhom DCE ACCOUNT -> nut "Language".
   Chon ngon ngu roi bam Dong y, sau do DONG VA MO LAI Excel.

2. CAC FILE TRONG THU MUC NAY
   vi.txt   Tieng Viet (ban goc, de doi chieu)
   en.txt   Tieng Anh
   zh.txt   Tieng Trung
   Moi dong co dang:   <chuoi tieng Viet goc><TAB><ban dich>
   Dong bat dau bang # la ghi chu.
   Dong dau file co  #name<TAB>Ten hien thi  -> ten hien ra trong hop chon ngon ngu.

3. SUA KHI DICH SAI
   Mo file bang Notepad++ / VSCode (PHAI luu lai o dinh dang UTF-8).
   Sua phan sau dau TAB. KHONG duoc sua phan truoc dau TAB (do la khoa tra cuu).
   Bo trong phan dich  ->  phan mem tu dung tieng Viet, KHONG bao gio loi.

4. BAN VA RIENG - KHONG BI MAT KHI CAP NHAT PHAN MEM
   Dat ban sua cua ban vao thu muc  Custom   Vi du:  Language\Custom\en.txt
   File trong Custom duoc nap SAU nen se GHI DE len file goc.
   Ban cap nhat phan mem chi de len en.txt / zh.txt, KHONG dung toi Custom.

5. THEM NGON NGU MOI - KHONG CAN SUA CODE
   Copy en.txt thanh ja.txt (hoac ma bat ky), dich lai, sua dong #name.
   Mo lai Excel -> ngon ngu moi tu dong xuat hien trong danh sach.

6. CHUOI CHUA DICH
   Khi chay, nhung chuoi chua co ban dich duoc ghi ra:
   Language\ThieuBanDich\<ma>_thieu.txt
   Ban co the dich bo sung roi dan vao file Custom.

7. LUU Y AN TOAN
   Co mot so chu CO Y KHONG dich vi chung vua hien thi vua duoc phan mem
   dung lam gia tri so sanh / ghi vao o Excel. Dich chung se lam SAI KET QUA.
   Nhung chuoi do khong co trong file nay.

8. CAM DICH (co bang chung trong ma nguon - dich vao se lam SAI KET QUA)
   Dong                      -> nut Chung_LicenseKhaDung (khong nam trong danh sach duoc dich)
   Cac chuoi co XUONG DONG trong giao dien (vi du "Lay ma may\r\nhien tai") khong tra duoc
   bang file nay - dong trong file khong the chua xuong dong that; de trong la dung.

9. KIEM TRA SAU KHI SUA
   Mo form o 100% va 150% (Settings > Display > Scale). Chu dai hon ban tieng Viet
   co the bi cat / de len o ben canh -> rut gon ban dich, KHONG sua kich thuoc form.
