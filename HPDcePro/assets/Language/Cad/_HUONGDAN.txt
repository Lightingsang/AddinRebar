HUONG DAN SUA BAN DICH - DCE Pro (AutoCAD / ZWCAD / GstarCAD / BricsCAD / progeCAD)
=========================================================================

1. CAC FILE TRONG THU MUC NAY  (Language\Cad\)
   vi.txt   Tieng Viet (ban goc, de doi chieu)
   en.txt   Tieng Anh
   zh.txt   Tieng Trung
   Moi dong co dang:   <chuoi goc><TAB><ban dich>
   Dong bat dau bang # la ghi chu. Phan dich de TRONG -> phan mem dung chuoi goc.

2. SUA KHI DICH SAI
   Mo bang Notepad++ / VSCode, luu UTF-8. CHI sua phan SAU dau TAB.
   Ban sua rieng nen dat o  Language\Cad\Custom\<ma>.txt  (nap sau, ghi de, khong mat khi cap nhat).

3. CAM DICH - TUYET DOI GIU TRONG (ke ca trong Custom)
   MC 1-1   MC 2-2   MC 3-3
      -> Form cau thang doc lai tieu de tab va so voi "MC 1-1"... Dich = loi NullReference, thoat lenh.
   Kata-DBIM
   RibbonRow   RibbonRow2   RibbonRow3   (khoa bo cuc hang ribbon, khong phai chu hien thi)
   Etabs  Safe  Sap  None  2D  3D  Column  Wall  Beam  Slab  Pile  Point  Beam Loads
      -> Palette CSI dung chinh chu nay de chon layer / che do ket noi.
   Ten Ck   va moi tu khoa dong lenh (Keywords) nhu Setup / Continue tren dong lenh CAD
      -> dong lenh CAD so sanh dung chu tieng Anh.

4. RIBBON
   AutoCAD / ZWCAD / BricsCAD: chu tren ribbon lay tu file nay (dong lenh khong doi).
   GstarCAD / progeCAD: ribbon nap tu file CUIX (DBimApps.cuix) nen KHONG doi theo ngon ngu.

5. CHUOI CHUA DICH
   Luc chay, chuoi chua co ban dich duoc ghi vao  Cad\ThieuBanDich\<ma>_thieu.txt
