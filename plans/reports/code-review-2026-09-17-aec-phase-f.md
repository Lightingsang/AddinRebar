## Score: 5 / 10

The planar-graph core is right where it was probed: crossings, T-junctions, collinear overlaps and coincident duplicates cut and merge into one edge with both handles; a zig-zag (a wall crossing another twice) yields the four faces; a figure-8 pinch node gives two rooms; snapping cascades converge; every directed edge is walked exactly once so a face can neither repeat nor loop forever; 20 000 unit segments build and walk in 0.5 s; ids are stable on reversed input (pinned). The writes are two-phase in the right order (`SpaceId` → layer check → render every tag → `GetObject(ForWrite)` → `EnsureLayer` → append; `SetDatabaseDefaults` before `LayerId`; a refusal leaves the counter at 0 — the harness pins `count() == n_a`), the seeds are thin shims, the read caps are pinned, and the shared `EnsureLayer` is byte-equivalent to the two copies it replaced. Held back by five things that hit the tool's own target inputs: the loop finder has no notion of an opening, so any plan whose walls stop at doors yields zero rooms (single-line) or one merged room per floor (double-line with jambs) (H1); two correctly tiled room outlines are a `room_overlap` warning because `SpatialRelation.Overlaps` counts a shared boundary run, so a plan drawn with outlines gets one warning per adjacent pair (H2); the `Nested` rule drops the room for any inner enclosure — a closet hung off the wall by one partition, a shaft in a hall — and reports only the closet (H3); `arch_auto_dimension_plan` at `MaxDimensions` 200 serialises to 64–66 KB, over the budget and over the cap with 7-digit coordinates, with the dimensions committed (H4, third phase in a row for a write tool); and the overshoot rule silently swallows a real gap whenever the nearest wall's foot coincides with the chain's junction (a jamb 100 mm from a partition end) while it reports an angled overshoot as `open_boundary` critical (H5).

## Unresolved (user decision)
