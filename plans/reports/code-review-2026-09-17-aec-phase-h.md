## Score: 5.5 / 10

Sound where it was probed: crossings, collinear overlaps and containment read as hard clashes with the right wording and a real `locationMm`; same-set pairs once by ordered handle, never an entity against itself; `CL-nnnn` after `AuditIssue.Ordered`; a straight route through a wall line / a double-line wall outline gets one opening at the crossing / the chord midpoint turned along the wall, a route ending inside gets none; the write is two-phase (layer, space, sizes and the whole plan checked before anything is `ForWrite`; `SetDatabaseDefaults` before layer/colour; the layer created only when something is drawn; locked/frozen → `Refused`); envelopes at every cap under budget (clash page 80 = 52.9 KB / 54.2 KB at 12-char handles, apply 100 = 40.9 KB, preview 100 = 45.9 KB); seeds thin shims whose `limit.maximum` and every schema key are pinned; files ≤ 151 lines; no magic numbers (`OpeningSizes`, named colour/offset constants). Held back by two semantic defects on the tools' default path: **contact is a critical hard clash** — every beam end on a column face, every pipe tee and end-to-end joint, so a well-drawn frame or pipe network checked against itself (the seed's own example 3) is nothing but criticals (H1); and **the opening planner only sees proper crossings** — a route with a vertex on a wall face, or crossing through an outline vertex, loses its pass silently, and with two passes the entry/exit pairing slips and draws a phantom opening between them (H2). Plus containment by area types (every MEP run "lies inside" the slab and the room, critical — M1), a clearance `locationMm` metres from the gap (M2), slab / in-wall chords read as passes (M3), a narrow phase whose cost the pair cap does not bound (M4), a documented type that does not exist (M5) and cross-space pairing (M6).
